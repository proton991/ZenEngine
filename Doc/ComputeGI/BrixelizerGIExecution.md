## Milestone 2 completion: incremental refresh and local detail

**Incremental refresh.** A committed geometry edit no longer retires the published generation. `DistanceFieldScene` keeps tracing it while a replacement builds from an immutable snapshot of the latest revision; a build in flight completes before the next starts, so continuous edits still publish. Each generation records the committed instance bounds and revisions at its snapshot. `UpdateStaleRegions` turns the old and new bounds of every edited instance into stale boxes, expanded by the field's hit band, a full trilinear stencil diagonal and one R8 step (`B·(2·tol + √3/7 + 1/255)`). More than eight boxes merge into their union; a changed instance list stales the whole field. Traces stop at the first stale box along the ray: an earlier hit is a result, and a covered miss that reaches the box is unavailable. Only a new scene identity discards the published generation. A refresh that cannot fit beside the published generation within the persistent budget replaces it instead, as a scene change does, rather than leaving a stale field in place indefinitely.

**Local detail volumes.** The 64-cell New Sponza field samples about every 8 cm, so each lantern closes into a solid blob. Bounce rays could not leave or enter the fixtures: lamps-only floor bounce was 89% short of the CUDA reference (milestone 6 table). `DistanceFieldScene::SetLocalVolumes` takes up to 32 world boxes. After the scene field publishes, each box is built in turn as a 32-cell field from the same committed geometry and workspace, then copied into a shared pool: one cell map and hierarchy per slot, plus a 160-texel tile of a 3D atlas with up to eight slots per row. A pass binds the pool once (`BindCompositeInputs`). The composite trace marches the scene field and switches to a volume's field inside its authority box: the core expanded by the same guard as a stale region, which keeps scene-field surfaces inside the core from causing hits outside the box. Receiver offsets use the brick size of the level at the receiver, so thin fixture parts are not stepped over. A volume that is unbuilt, failed or touched by an edit falls back to the scene field. A moved volume rebuilds alone. `VoxelGIRenderer` places one volume around every enabled point and spot light with nonzero intensity, three radiance-cache voxels each way. The visibility generation includes the volumes' revision, so gathers and the sky cache restart when a volume is published or invalidated.

The pool is allocated only after the scene field publishes. Without volumes it is a minimal placeholder; a pool that cannot be allocated leaves every volume on the scene field. Its contents are defined once at creation, so passes bind it without unknown-initialization warnings. Construction reuses the scene field's workspace (its per-cell arrays are larger) and releases the shared geometry snapshot only after the last volume is copied. `IsRefiningField` reports a published field that predates committed edits or still builds volumes. Scripted captures and profiles count those frames as warm-up, like preparation. The capture metadata records `field_refining`, `field_local_volumes` and `field_local_volumes_ready`.

`DistanceFieldLocalVolumeOpensSlit` builds a 1.2 cm slit inside the scene field's hit band in front of a wall. The scene field closes the slit; with a local volume around it, the composite trace reaches the wall within 0.3 bricks. Moving the volume falls back to the scene field until the rebuild publishes, and an oversized set is rejected without disturbing the published volume. It passes in all four execution modes. The owner suite also covers publication across edits (`CompleteBuiltField` now waits until the published generation matches the committed geometry), budget-limited refreshes, and empty-scene edits.

## Milestone 3: GI integration

Once prepared, Compute GI traces only the distance field. The sky cache (`VoxelSkyIrradianceField[Counts]SP`), probe diffuse and specular traces (`ProbeTraceFieldSP`, `ProbeSpecularTraceFieldSP`), and pixel gathers (`HybridTrace*FieldSP`, including the half-resolution and fallback variants) bind `BindCompositeInputs`: the scene field, its stale regions and the local volumes. Mixed providers use HW-plus-field variants (`HybridTraceHardwareFieldSP`, `ProbeTraceHardwareFieldSP` and their specular and counting forms). Every consumer keeps the field's three results distinct. Unavailable samples are excluded from estimators and renormalized over valid samples; a probe with no valid sample is published empty, and an invalid specular sample counts as an untraced block. A sky-cache cell with every sample unavailable publishes kind 3, which radiance injection writes as alpha 0. Owner-triangle receivers in the sky cache still read scene geometry for their surface point, not for visibility.

Near-light visibility at hits now traces the finite hit-to-light segment through the field, including local volumes and occluders outside the radiance cache. The endpoint is excluded by one ULP, and visibility-only rays omit gradient evaluation. Per-cell light-cache injection and direct lighting use shadow maps in the forced-Compute configuration. Ray-traced direct shadows are an HW-only option; an explicit software shadow selection migrates to maps. The older two-voxel field-shadow experiment below is historical; the current three-voxel default restores field visibility and is being requalified.

`BuildRayQueryGraph` prepares the field whenever GI renders. While it is pending or after it fails, an already active HW path traces every feature (`field_preparing`, `field_failed`). Cold Compute now renders a voxel-cone GI preview with shadow maps while the field is pending, rather than waiting in PBR. The preview uses the current scene's radiance voxels and voxel sky visibility; it never samples the unfinished field. Field publication starts ray/probe gathering with fresh history and regenerates sky visibility. Failed preparation without a usable HW path still reports the PBR fallback. The visibility generation combines the field serial, the local-volume revision and the scene generation; gather histories and the sky cache restart when it changes.

## Milestone 4: switching and lifetime

Opposite errors must not cancel into a pass, so the absolute errors and their distribution were inspected as well. With three-voxel volumes the lamps-only absolute error matches HW RT, whose small floor mean is itself a cancellation.
