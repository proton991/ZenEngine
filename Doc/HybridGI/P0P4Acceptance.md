### Steady bounce history experiment, recorded before measurement

Before the experiment, composed diffuse fails kloppenheim hall at 25.136% P99 and hotel hall at 20.391%. Hotel has no unexpected zeros once bounce is included. Normalizing each channel's error by the composed reference mean, hotel's sky error alone has P99 16.920%, bounce alone 9.131%, and their sum 20.391%. The mean error remains only −0.164%.

Test using the configured 32-frame history limit for steady diffuse bounce, whose implementation currently caps it at 16. Keep the eight-frame responsive limit, correlated cache-change update, sky/specular histories, four-ray count and all quality limits unchanged. This is a reconstruction change from 16 to 32 steady bounce frames, not a larger global history preset. Its purpose is to reduce static bounce variance without slowing the required eight-frame lighting response. Adopt only with measured improvement and passing bounce/light-change regression gates; otherwise restore the current shader. Kloppenheim remains dominated by sky error and is a separate blocker.

**Result: adopted.** A fresh ten-view sweep passes **9/10**, with every floor passing. Hotel hall improves to **19.591% P99**, zero unexpected zeros, −0.154% bias and 4.659% RMS. Kloppenheim hall remains at **25.002% P99**. The independent point-room and thin-wall bounce biases are −4.517% and −4.774%, within 10%; ray-bounce thin-wall leakage is **0.00813%**, within 1%. Every frame-8 move/off/emission gate and frame-64 continuous-light gate passes. The tightest light-change result is thin-wall move, P99 **19.432%**. Sky history stays intact. The cone-bounce comparison still leaks through the thin wall (23.7%); it is a reported diagnostic, not the selected hardware path.

Artifacts: `hardware/static-results.json` (before), `hardware-steady32/static-results.json` (after), and `bounce-regression/bounce-results.json`. The new steady-bounce shader validates for Vulkan 1.2. Independent sky evidence above is from the committed checkpoint; the changed temporal branch only handles ray-hit bounce and was inactive in those isolated-sky captures.
