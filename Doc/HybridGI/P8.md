## Active implementation

Compute uses four residual rays, with two extra guided rays on reset frames, receivers where the previous sky mean is positive, and unoccupied starts with positive angular guide support. Hardware sky uses four residual rays. Both keep the source-ray allocation, one specular ray, full receiver resolution, 32-frame histories and five filter iterations. The angular guide is 8×8 per cube face. Hardware retains a radiance-only component in the proposal so cells crossing the horizon remain sampleable; cell masses and evaluated densities include the same term. Compute retains its qualified cosine proposal.

The budget uses the **resolved sky provider**, including mixed routing and hardware fallback. Reference accumulation retains its configured count. Captures and profiles record the effective budget. Executable/shaders: `build/p8/archives/dark-exploration/`.

Retained changes:

- Cache proposal row weights, reduce fully visible rows with weighted direction sums, and reuse conditional sampling densities.
- Specialize the five filter scales, skip background/unneeded variance work, and reuse full-precision current positions from the temporal pass.
- Cache voxel occupied-start classification in sky-guide alpha.
- Build conservative 8×8 sky-support masks and position bounds. The bounds cost 12.44 MB at 1080p and reject only taps that cannot satisfy the existing normal/plane tests.
- Queue unresolved final-filter pixels and evaluate each original 65×65 footprint with a 256-thread workgroup. Preserve identity, normal, plane, occupied-start, Gaussian and range tests; proven-zero tiles retain their denominator contribution. Parallel summation changes floating-point addition order.
- Count actually issued receiver queries in separate diagnostic shader variants. Timed production shaders contain no counters. Cache and analytic-shadow queries still need coverage. (Added 2026-10-17; see the [close-out](P8Closeout.md#issued-rays).)

No hierarchy or reduced-resolution tracing experiment is enabled.

- **Cost scales with sky rays.** The two adaptive rays cost 3.9 / 6.2 ms on Compute (2.5 / 3.2 ms per ray per pixel), against 0.34 / 0.33 ms on HW RT. Per extra ray, Compute is 12× slower in the hall and 19× from above. Bounce and reflections reuse the sky and specular rays, so turning them off saves almost nothing. Closest-hit sky rays, needed by ray bounce, cost 4.4 ms on Compute from above and nothing measurable in the hall or on HW RT.
- **Software traversal is divergent and latency-bound, not bandwidth-bound.** On Compute, instructions average 12.3 / 10.5 active threads of 32, against 25 on HW RT. Warp occupancy is 26 / 23%, and 32 / 24% of warp samples wait on L1TEX loads. L1 hit rate is 85–89%, L2 read hits are about 98% and L2 throughput is only 8–13%. From above, 26.5% of samples wait at workgroup barriers: each 256-thread group waits for its slowest rays before learning the guide (10% on HW RT). The stackless escape-link loop visits nodes in a fixed order and interleaves box and triangle tests across lanes.
- **Sky search.** Compute's tiled layout takes 1.69 ms in the hall: 30% occupancy and 44% of samples at barriers. HW RT's packed indirect layout takes 0.76 ms at 96% occupancy. The tiled layout was chosen on 2026-10-14 from 1080p moving-light FPS. This static 960×540 profile favors the packed layout, but that is not yet a measured Compute result. The final filter and the C++ dispatch both choose the layout from the sky provider.
- **Shared passes.** The five filters (1.45–1.84 ms) and temporal pass are the same on both tiers. On HW RT they cost as much as `HybridTrace`; see the HW RT profile below.

### HW RT profile

Same build, workload and runner, all features on HW RT, plus 1920×1080 runs (the P8 target extent; `--width 1920 --height 1080`). Rays per pixel match Compute exactly in every variant. | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Hall, 960×540 | 4.90 | 4.40 | 1.59 | 1.56 | 0.76 | 0.19 | 0.19 | 0.06 |
| Top, 960×540 | 3.59 | 3.59 | 1.54 | 1.45 | 0.03 | 0.24 | 0.22 | 0.07 |

- **At 1080p the HW RT frame is already at the 16.7 ms target at base clock.** The filters cost as much as `HybridTrace`, and the GI passes scale with pixel count (3.3–4.3×). Boost-clock 1080p times were not measured.
- **`HybridTrace` scales only with the sky ray count.** It costs 0.20 / 0.16 ms per sky ray per pixel, plus about 0.45 / 0.37 ms fixed at 960×540 (a fit through the baseline, adaptive-off and two-sample runs). Nearest-hit vs any-hit, reflections and specular each change it by at most 0.01 ms.
- **`HybridTrace` doesn't keep the RT cores busy.** They are active 32–37% of the time, and only 15–19% of warp samples wait on them. Warp occupancy is 31%, with 64% of warp slots empty while the SM is active. Another 22% of samples wait on L1TEX, with about 5% texture L1 hits on environment and radiance-cache lookups, and 10% wait at the guide-learning barriers. Each thread also keeps the 16-entry guide `rows[]` array live through the ray loop.
- **The filters are memory-latency bound.** Iteration 0 has 74% of warp samples waiting on L1TEX and pushes L2 to 74% of peak. It costs 1.6× a later iteration because every one of its 25 taps rebuilds variance from moments, bounce moments, reflection history and history length. The 16-pixel-step iteration hits L1 only 6% of the time. Each tap fetches about ten textures; occupancy is 40%.
- **The sky search matters only in the hall**: 2.51 ms at 1080p, with 97% occupancy, 60% of samples waiting on L1TEX and L2 at 55% of peak. From above it costs 0.04 ms.
- **The temporal pass is DRAM-bound.** 78–82% of samples wait on L1TEX, with a 75% L2 hit rate. It reads and writes ten history textures, about 120 bytes per pixel each way: `Sky`, `Moments`, `Position`, `Bounce` and `Reflection` are RGBA32F.

## Rejected experiments

The occupancy max-mip hierarchy passed exact first-cell, normal and position comparisons at 64³/128³/256³ in four submission modes, plus 578 core and 20 native voxel tests. Both loop forms were slower: 52.48/51.53 FPS in the hall. It was removed; source and native test binaries are in `build/p8/archives/hierarchy-rejected/`. A driver shader-compiler crash was isolated to `textureQueryLevels`; deriving mip count from volume size passed. Temporary diagnostics were removed.

The half-resolution prototype was slower (48 FPS) and failed hall P99 (32.711% versus 20%). Four-ray compute sampling with the compact guide reached about 67 FPS but failed two shipping views. Packed search buffers, smaller workgroups, coarse quadrature and alternate guide lookup schemes did not improve performance. Details: `build/p8/rejected-trace-experiments.json`.

Early timings with an invalid reserved descriptor set are excluded (`build/p8/invalid-measurements.json`). The earlier serial hall diagnostic took 88.028 ms median GPU time, about 11.24 FPS; it already contained guide arithmetic changes and is not an untouched-renderer baseline.
