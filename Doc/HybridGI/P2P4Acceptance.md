## What was measured

The mixed-origin failure produced six unexpected black pixels when only specular occlusion selected HW RT. The independent fix removed them. Additional switch sequences exposed all-miss histories on a narrow bevel: each final sparse footprint contained only its center, missing the nearest lit compatible pixels. Variance bootstrapping did not fix this and was discarded. The last iteration now searches its existing 65×65 footprint densely for sky only when the accumulated sky and temporal mean are exactly zero. Identity, plane, geometric/shading normal, occupied-start and Gaussian range tests still apply. No artificial light floor or relaxed acceptance limit is used. The exact frame-96 reproduction falls from two unexpected zeros to zero (RMS 5.138%, P99 17.563%). All 30 final switch cases pass.

The final shader also passes 16 focused fixture runs across both providers: deferred, forward, single-sided, blend and transmission closed rooms remain dark, black-environment output remains zero, and narrow-slot/thin-pole captures pass the existing finite/composition checks. Fresh shipping captures pass all ten required compute views and five hardware top views; all fifteen flagpole regions are rechecked on those final hardware images. These checks supplement, rather than relabel, the archived hardware hall and motion measurements.

Compute convergence compares unfiltered 4096-sample captures accumulated over 32 frames (131,072 residual rays/pixel) with matching 1024-sample captures accumulated over 64 frames (65,536). The limits remain 1% absolute mean bias and 3% RMS over all receivers and floor regions. Unused specular and bounce are disabled in this sky-only check; the sky estimator and voxel geometry are unchanged. Shipping quality retains its separate four-ray / 32-history / five-filter-iteration evidence.

The flagpole test keeps the three approved 960×540 rectangles intersected with the frozen floor mask, the unchanged independent Mitsuba dataset and its surface-agreement rules. Independent captures remove normal maps; shipping captures retain them and compare composed diffuse with the matching converged reference. Both references use 1024 samples over 64 frames. Region limits remain 1% bias / 3% excess RMS for the independent comparison and 2% bias / 8% RMS / 20% P99 / zero unexpected zeros for shipping. The new validator initially omitted the original floor mask and incorrectly gated visible pole/wall surfaces; it now shares the original mask helper, with receiver counts checked against the archived report. The unrestricted-crop failure is retained as a diagnostic.

The backend checks use the new explicit configuration selectors and record requested/effective hardware feature masks. Each static image is checked against its selected sky provider's converged reference, and specular visibility is checked against that provider's homogeneous endpoint at identical frame/sample indices (2% absolute RMS/P99 tolerance, fixed before measurement). Linear composition and finite values are also checked. Switch captures exercise masks 0, 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 63, 62, 61, 59, 55, 47, 31 and 0; every first-frame sky history is exactly one. The half-wall fixture changes only sky-cache visibility while keeping cone bounce, isolating that selector's image contribution.

The selected mixed cases cover each feature in both directions. They complement the archived environment, analytic and motion gates; they do not claim an exhaustive environment × backend-combination matrix or P8 qualification.

The comparison and ground-truth readers accept these archives. `engine.cfg` is restored transactionally; inherited explicit backend settings are removed from each temporary capture configuration before applying its frozen selections.
